Dynamik

Keep walking

Introducing Dynamik

- From the Greek Move/Moving.

Turn your daily walks into a game with personalized challenges, custom goals, and smart tracking powered by Apple Intelligence.

Dynamik is designed to elevate both your physical and mental fitness by guiding you through an engaging, tailored health journey.

Think different

With Apple Intelligence as your 24/7 personal trainer. It stays with you every step of the way, delivering smart summaries of your long-term progress while actively highlighting key areas of improvement to keep you moving forward.

Move beyond standard tracking

Along with monitoring your distance, steps, and streaks, Dynamik lets you follow creative paths to draw shapes directly on the map. Share your custom routes with friends, or go to the Explore tab to download and walk routes designed by others.

Getting started is seamless

With full Game Center integration, there are no tedious sign-up forms or accounts to create. Just download the app and start playing your fitness journey.

Your data stays yours

Privacy is built into the foundation of Dynamik. Location tracking is entirely optional, you can enjoy the app with or without it. If you choose to enable location permissions, your data is completely secure. It is never stored, never shared, and never leaves your device.
