Eraser

Clean Any Webpage

Click to hide any element.

Take total control of your browsing experience.

If you can see it, you can erase it.

Eraser is a lightweight Safari App Extension designed to instantly declutter any webpage.

Unlike traditional ad blockers, Eraser lets you choose exactly what stays and what goes. With just a click, you can vanish any intrusive, distracting, or overwhelming web element in real time.

Perfect for neurodivergent individuals experiencing sensory overload, professionals needing deep focus, or anyone tired of modern web clutter.

What can you erase?

  • Autoplay Videos and GIFs: Stop looping animations that break your focus.
  • Cookie Prompts and Paywalls: Dismiss annoying GDPR overlays obstructing your view.
  • Intrusive Ads and Pop-ups: Clear out visual noise effortlessly.
  • Distressing Content: Temporarily hide images or text that cause anxiety or discomfort.

Advanced Control and Customization

Tailor how you clean your web pages with built-in configurations:

  • Smart Activation: Set Eraser to turn off automatically after your first click, or leave it active to clean multiple elements in a row.
  • Layout Modes: Choose to remove an item and let the text auto-rearrange, or hide the item while preserving the original page layout to prevent shifting.
  • Instant Undo: Erase something by mistake? Bring it back immediately with a simple undo.

100% Privacy-First and Local

Your data belongs to you. Eraser operates entirely on your Mac. It does not track your browsing history, remember your hidden elements across sessions, or send data off your device.

Completely Free

No premium tiers, no subscriptions, and absolutely no ads. Just a cleaner, calmer internet.
