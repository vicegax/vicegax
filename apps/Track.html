Introducing Track

- From the Greek Move/Moving.

Track your daily walks and challenge others.

Track combines smart step tracking with GPS/Map art, allowing you to draw custom shapes directly on the map as you move.

Think different

Whether you want to sketch a heart on Valentine's Day or follow a route designed by someone else, Track turns standard movement into an engaging, visual health journey.

Move beyond standard tracking

Along with monitoring your distance, steps, and streaks, Track lets you follow creative paths to draw shapes directly on the map. Share your custom routes with friends, or go to the Explore tab to download and walk routes designed by others.

Getting started is seamless

With full Game Center integration, there are no tedious sign-up forms or accounts to create. Just download the app and start playing your fitness journey.

Your data stays yours

Privacy is built into the foundation of Track. Location tracking is entirely optional, you can enjoy the app with or without it. If you choose to enable location permissions, your data is completely secure. It is never stored, never shared, and never leaves your device.
