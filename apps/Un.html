Un

LinkedIn Safari Web Extension

Description

Safari Web Extension for LinkedIn

un is a set of handy tools to make your experience on LinkedIn more efficient, and more bearable.

un Promote

Remove all invasive and annoying ads, called promoted jobs. Most of the time those jobs are unrelated to your job search and they are just cluttering your experience, filter them out automatically.

un Follow

Are you receiving a lot of spam in your feed? Unfollow every one at once by just tapping on a button, this extension will take care of the rest.

un Apply

Do you want to keep track of jobs that you have already applied? Surprisingly, LinkedIn being a social media with the sole purpose of finding a job, doesn't actually provide any tool or insights into your applications, it doesn't even let you register that you already did. But this extension helps you keep track of that.

un Filter

LinkedIn should provide proper filters for job searches, for example when you are not interested on seeing job posts from a specific company, you should be able to remove that company from your searches. But LinkedIn user experience is bad by definition, so this extension helps you correct that. Select a company that you want to filter out and you will not see it again.
